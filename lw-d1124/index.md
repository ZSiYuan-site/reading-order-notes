## How to use State Law Watch well

1. Open the page that matches your question and read the top block first.
2. Check the date or the heading to confirm it is the version you want.
3. Use the [new laws by effective date](https://laws.resuhive.com/) home page when you are not sure which page to start with.

When two pages seem to cover the same topic, compare the dates and the headings. The newer page and the one with the more specific heading are usually the better starting point, and the other one makes a good second read.

Every page on State Law Watch is built to answer one question directly, so you do not have to scroll through long introductions. Open the page, read the first block, and you will usually have your answer before the rest of the page loads. If you need more detail, the headings below the first block break the topic into smaller parts.
