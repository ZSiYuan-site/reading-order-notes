# Game Codes for Mobile Games: Where the Working List Comes From

A working gift code is worth a few minutes of searching, but a list that mixed live and expired codes would waste more time than it saves. This guide explains how one reward site builds and maintains its code lists, what the entries actually tell you, and how to redeem a code without the usual mistakes. It is written for players who want the routine, not a long list of codes that will be stale next week.

## Where the codes come from

Daily Reward Links says its codes are taken from official channels and re-checked several times a day. The [home page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/) currently shows 56 working codes across 17 games, with the last check at 09:23 UTC on November 19, 2026. That re-checking is the important part: a code can be live in the morning and invalid by the evening, so a static article is not enough.

## What a code entry contains

Take the [Genshin Impact codes page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/genshin-impact/codes/) as an example. It lists five working codes, each with its reward and a Copy button. The newest, MIJIQ23V2437, gives 10,000 Mora, 10 Adventurer's Experience, 5 Fine Enhancement Ore, 5 Jueyun Chili Chicken and 5 Stir-Fried Fish Noodles. Two others, PJ2KEBRAYSRM and Y6JYMKV6JKSL, give 60 Primogems each. Below the active codes is a separate expired list, including KeepUpWithMitya and EPIC2026. Keeping the two lists apart is what makes the page usable.

## Games with large code lists

Some titles move faster than others. The [Tacticus codes page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/tacticus/codes/) tracks 24 working codes, the most of any game on the site, with SUPERTACTICS offering 2,000 Coins, 30 Blackstone and 10 Aleph Shards. The [Arknights Endfield codes page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/arknights-endfield/codes/) has four, including ENDFIELDSTEAM for 8,000 T-Creds and other items. Smaller lists, such as the two codes for Neverness to Everness, are quicker to scan.

## Redeeming without mistakes

Codes are case-sensitive and most work once per account. Copy the code rather than typing it, then check it is not in the expired list. For Genshin Impact, reach Adventure Rank 10, open the Paimon menu, then Settings, Account and Redeem Now, or use the official gift page on the web with your server region and character nickname. Rewards arrive in the in-game mailbox.

## Watching end dates

A few codes carry official deadlines. The site flags ENDFIELDSTEAM as ending November 25, 2026, 2026BD2NOV for Brown Dust 2 ending November 30, and ADIDASxPOKEMON for Pokemon GO ending January 15, 2027. Those dated entries are listed on the [home page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/) as well as on each game's page.

## One daily check

The practical routine is short: open the games index, pick the titles you play, copy the active codes and ignore the expired ones. The [Wuthering Waves codes page](https://rewards.newgamescout.com/wuthering-waves/codes/) follows the same layout, so once you have read one page you can read them all.
