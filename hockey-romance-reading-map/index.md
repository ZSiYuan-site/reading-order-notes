# Hockey romance reading map

Two hockey romance series keep coming up in reading-order questions: Rachel Reid's Game Changers books, which many readers know by the title Heated Rivalry, and Elle Kennedy's Off-Campus books. They share a sport and a habit of giving each book to a new couple, but the reading advice is quite different.

## Game Changers: start at book one, not book two

The [Heated Rivalry (Game Changers) books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/series/heated-rivalry-books-in-order/) page shows six published books and one more, Unrivaled, dated June 1, 2027. The confusing part is that the series is named after its second book. Game Changer (2018) follows Scott Hunter and Kip Grady, and Heated Rivalry (March 25, 2019) is the one with Shane Hollander and Ilya Rozanov. Tough Guy, Common Goal and Role Model then give other couples their own book, and The Long Game (2022) comes back to Shane and Ilya. If you saw the screen adaptation and want the original, you can start at book two, but publication order gives you the side characters before they matter.

## Off-Campus: five books, one college

Elle Kennedy's series is more compact. The [Off-Campus books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/series/off-campus-books-in-order/) list has five books at Briar University: The Deal (2015), The Mistake (2015), The Score (2016), The Goal (2016) and The Legacy Collection (2021), which gathers four couples from the earlier books. Each of the first four is a stand-alone romance with the same friend group around it, so a reader who only wants one couple can pick by pairing: Hannah and Garrett, Grace and Logan, Allie and Dean, Sabrina and Tucker.

## Which one first?

- Pick Game Changers if you want a series that is still growing, with a dated next book.
- Pick Off-Campus if you want a finished set you can read in a long weekend.
- Read both in publication order if you are not sure. Neither series punishes you for it.

Every list above comes from the [Books in Order](https://books.resuhive.com/) site, which rebuilds its reading orders every Monday so the upcoming dates stay current. If you need a different author, there is a full [authors page](https://books.resuhive.com/authors/) with each author's books in order.
