## How to use Books in Order well

1. Open the page that matches your question and read the top block first.
2. Check the date or the heading to confirm it is the version you want.
3. Use the [books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/) home page when you are not sure which page to start with.

A good habit is to save the one or two pages you use most and check them on the same day each week. Information on books.resuhive.com is refreshed regularly, so a page you looked at last week may have a new entry or a changed date today.
