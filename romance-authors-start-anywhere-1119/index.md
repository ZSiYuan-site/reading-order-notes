# Romance Authors Where You Can Start Anywhere: Emily Henry, Abby Jimenez and Kennedy Ryan

Romance readers ask a different question from thriller readers. They rarely want to know which book is first; they want to know whether it matters. For these three authors the answer is different each time, so here is a short map based on the Books in Order pages.

## Emily Henry: ten books, no series

The [Emily Henry books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/emily-henry-books-in-order/) page lists 10 published books and 10 standalones, with none requiring another. The adult romances run Beach Read (2020), People We Meet on Vacation (2021), Book Lovers (2022), Happy Place (2023), Funny Story (2024) and Great Big Beautiful Life (April 22, 2025). The next, Modern Classic, is dated April 6, 2027. Start with Beach Read, but skipping around costs you nothing.

## Abby Jimenez: three series and three standalones

The [Abby Jimenez books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/abby-jimenez-books-in-order/) page lists 12 books. The Friend Zone series has three connected contemporary romances: The Friend Zone (2019), The Happy Ever After Playlist (2020) and Life's Too Short (2021). The Part of Your World series has four entries including a short follow-up novella and a free bonus chapter. Her next book, Feels Like Falling, is dated April 20, 2027. Here, order matters more, because the series share characters.

## Kennedy Ryan: seven series, twenty-one books

The [Kennedy Ryan books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/kennedy-ryan-books-in-order/) page lists 21 books across 7 series. The earliest, The Bennetts, starts with When You Are Mine (2014), then Loving You Always (2014), Be Mine Forever (2015) and Until I'm Yours (2016). Her Soul trilogy begins with My Soul to Keep (2015). No new book has been announced.

## Which one is right for you

- Want no homework: Emily Henry. Pick any title.
- Want a connected group of characters: Abby Jimenez, in publication order.
- Want a big backlist with several worlds: Kennedy Ryan, one series at a time.

## A practical tip for series readers

When a page marks a short book as a novella or a bonus chapter, read it right after the book it follows. These short pieces are easy to miss and they are often where a side character gets an ending.

## If you like the sports side of romance

Rachel Reid's hockey books have their own page at [Rachel Reid books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/rachel-reid-books-in-order/), and the [Heated Rivalry series page](https://books.resuhive.com/series/heated-rivalry-books-in-order/) shows which book comes first, since the series is named after its second book. The next Reid book, Unrivaled, is dated June 1, 2027.
