# Christmas Coloring Pages by Theme: A Map of 16 Sets

Choosing a coloring page is much faster when you can see the whole shelf at once. A long, unsorted gallery makes you scroll past dozens of pictures you do not want; a theme index turns the same choice into two clicks. This guide maps the sixteen themes available on ColorPageHub and explains which sets are big enough for a group and which are better for a quiet afternoon at home.

## The big four

The [all themes index](https://coloring.resuhive.com/themes/) lists Santa at 14 pages, Christmas Tree at 14, Snowman at 13 and Reindeer at 10. Those four sets make up a large share of the [111 Christmas coloring pages](https://coloring.resuhive.com/christmas-coloring-pages/). If you need twenty different sheets for a class or a party, start here: you can take a mix of easy and detailed pages from a single theme and nobody gets the same picture.

## Middle-sized sets

Elf has 8 pages and Christmas Animals has 9, which is enough for a small group. The [Christmas animals coloring pages](https://coloring.resuhive.com/christmas-animals-coloring-pages/) set includes kittens with an ornament, a puppy in a Santa hat, a bear with a scarf and an owl on a branch. Gingerbread Man has 6 pages, Christmas Gnome has 6, Christmas Ornament has 6, and Christmas Wreath has 6. The [gingerbread man coloring pages](https://coloring.resuhive.com/gingerbread-man-coloring-pages/) are a reliable choice for younger children because the shapes are simple and familiar.

## Small sets worth knowing about

Candy Cane has 3 pages, Nutcracker 3, Gingerbread House 5, Christmas Stocking 4 and Nativity 4. The [candy cane coloring pages](https://coloring.resuhive.com/candy-cane-coloring-pages/) are a good example of a small set doing one thing well: a single object in several arrangements, from a plain cane to a jar of them. The [winter coloring pages](https://coloring.resuhive.com/winter-coloring-pages/) section adds Penguin with 10 pages alongside the snowmen, so it is a useful alternative when you want a Christmas-free option.

## How the pages are labelled

Every page carries an age range and a level. Santa Waving is marked Ages 5 to 10 and Easy, while Christmas Tree by the Fireplace is marked Adults and Detailed. The [free printable coloring pages](https://coloring.resuhive.com/) landing page mixes the levels so you can see the difference before you choose. All 121 pages on the site are described as hand-checked for clean lines and a white background, which matters if you are printing at home rather than tracing on screen.

## Printing and sharing

Print one page at a time, scaled to Letter or A4, or download a PDF of that single page. There is no sign-up and no email field. The [About page](https://coloring.resuhive.com/about/) states the pages are free for personal and classroom use. For a classroom, the simplest plan is to pick one large theme, print a different page for each child, and keep the detailed ones for early finishers.
