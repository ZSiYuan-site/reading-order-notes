# Tana French Books in Order: Two Series and One Standalone

Tana French is a useful author to read in order because her two series are short enough to finish and different enough to feel separate. The [Tana French books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/tana-french-books-in-order/) page, updated November 18, 2026, counts 10 published books across two series and one standalone, with no new title announced. Here is how the list is arranged and where a new reader should begin.

## Dublin Murder Squad: six books

The first series is the one most readers meet. It has six novels, each narrated by a different detective, and it begins with In the Woods in 2007. The order then runs The Likeness in 2008, Faithful Place in 2010, Broken Harbour in 2012, The Secret Place in 2014 and The Trespasser in 2016. The books share a setting but hand the spotlight to a new investigator, so publication order is the safest choice. The page marks In the Woods as the best place to start because it sets the tone.

## Cal Hooper: three books

The second series is a trilogy about a retired Chicago cop living in rural western Ireland. It has The Searcher in 2020, The Hunter in 2024 and The Keeper, dated March 31, 2026. The Keeper is the final book in the trilogy. Only three novels means this is a manageable commitment, and the gap between the first two is long enough that a re-read of The Searcher before The Hunter is a reasonable idea.

## The standalone

The Witch Elm was published in 2018. It sits outside both series and can be read at any point, though the author page lists it by year between The Trespasser and The Searcher. If you want the cleanest path, read the six Dublin Murder Squad books, then the standalone, then the Cal Hooper trilogy. That follows publication order with one small adjustment.

## If you want a longer spy series

A reader who finishes French and wants more may prefer [Mick Herron books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/mick-herron-books-in-order/). Herron has 23 books across two series and five standalones. The main Slough House sequence begins with Slow Horses in 2010 and has 14 entries including novellas, with Clown Town in 2025 and a collected volume, Watch the Quiet Snow, in October 2026. It is roughly twice the size of French's catalogue.

## Another option for mystery fans

For a classic police procedural, [John Sandford books in order](https://books.resuhive.com/john-sandford-books-in-order/) follows Minnesota detective Lucas Davenport. The lists are rebuilt every Monday, so publication dates and upcoming titles stay current. The [authors index](https://books.resuhive.com/authors/) is the quickest way to jump between all of them.
