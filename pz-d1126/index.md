## How to use Pips Answers well

1. Open the page that matches your question and read the top block first.
2. Check the date or the heading to confirm it is the version you want.
3. Use the [pips answers today](https://puzzles.newgamescout.com/) home page when you are not sure which page to start with.

Nothing here needs an account or a download. The pages load quickly on a phone, and the text is plain, so you can read it on a slow connection without waiting for images.
