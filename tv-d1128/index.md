## How to use Trivia Nook well

1. Open the page that matches your question and read the top block first.
2. Check the date or the heading to confirm it is the version you want.
3. Use the [trivia questions and answers](https://trivia.resuhive.com/) home page when you are not sure which page to start with.

If you are sharing a page with a friend or a family member, send the direct link rather than the home page. It saves them a step, and the page title tells them what they are about to read before they open it.

A good habit is to save the one or two pages you use most and check them on the same day each week. Information on trivia.resuhive.com is refreshed regularly, so a page you looked at last week may have a new entry or a changed date today.
