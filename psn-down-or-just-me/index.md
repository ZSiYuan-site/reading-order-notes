# Is PSN down or is it just me?

Every few weeks a PlayStation outage trends and the same questions come back: is it down for everyone, how long will it last, and why does my console say something different from the news. This note puts the answers in one place.

## What counts as down

PlayStation splits its network into services. Account management covers signing in and creating an account. Gaming and social covers online play, trophies and game help. PlayStation Store covers browsing and buying. Each can be listed as Outage or Degraded for one country at a time, so an outage in one region can leave another untouched. The [live PSN status page](https://tools.resuhive.com/is-psn-down) lets you pick your country and see only the services that have an open entry.

## Why status can look fine while you cannot play

PlayStation only publishes problems it has confirmed, and confirmation takes a few minutes. Your own network is the other common cause. Two quick checks help: run the console's connection test, and try another device on the same Wi-Fi. If the second device also fails, the router or provider is the likely problem.

## How long will it last

No one outside PlayStation knows, and PlayStation does not publish repair times. The honest answer is to reload the status page and watch for the entry to disappear. A page that promises a recovery time is guessing.

## Is it PSN or the game

Fortnite, Call of Duty, EA and 2K titles all run their own servers. If PSN shows working and one game fails, check that game's status page or its official account. The PSN page has a short list of where to look.

## Quick checklist

- Pick your country on the [PSN status tool](https://tools.resuhive.com/is-psn-down).
- If clear, run the console connection test.
- Restart console and router.
- Check NAT type and DNS.
- Check account and subscription.

Similar official-data checkers exist for [Is Apple Down?](https://tools.resuhive.com/is-apple-down) and the [FAA ground stop list](https://tools.resuhive.com/faa-ground-stop), and all of them are free on [OneJob Tools](https://tools.resuhive.com/).
